# Final User Journey

A cook saves a shawarma potato bowl recipe in Batch's My recipes section, then adds it to a collection and shares it with their friends. The original recipe includes a homemade seasoning mix, grilled or pan fried chicken, and roasted potatoes.

The author tries using premade shawarma seasoning instead of making the seasoning mix. After cooking and trying it, they add a cooking entry describing the substitution and note that it turned out great. The entry stays attached to the recipe without changing the original instructions for everyone with access.

The second cook opens the recipe from Shared and reads the author's attempt. They use the same premade sesaoning but air-fry the chicken instead of grilling or pan frying it. They record those changes, then add the result that the chicken stayed juicy in the air fryer.

The third cook reads the recipe and both attempts and follows the second cook's approach. They also airfry the potatoes with the chicken. After cooking, they record that the potatoes were crispy and absorbed a lot of the flavor from the chicken, and the chicken stayed juicy.

The author returns to the recipe and sees how their friends built on to the substitutions. All three attempts remain together, so anyone with access can compare the adjustments and results before deciding how to make their dish. They can still return to the original instructions, while having the cooking history attached to it which shows the variations that has been tried in case they want to go back to those attempts.

[Final User Journey Narrated](https://youtu.be/MkoYtk0NH3o) follows this journey quickly, going over the shared cooking history feature which is a key benefit of this app.
