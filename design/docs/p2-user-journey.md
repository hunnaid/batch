# P2 User Journey

A cook wants to make their mom's biryani recipe, but the recipe is spread across iMessage texts in Urdu
saved as screenshots, with extra tips they remember from conversations. Starting with an empty Batch database, they register an account, sign in, and create a collection called "Family Recipes." They manually enter the recipe with ingredients and ordered instructions, translating the text to English for ease of use. They add the verbal tips in instructions and type the source as their mother's recipe. While cooking, they use half the amount of chili and steam the rice for an extra 5 minutes, so they add a cooking entry describing those adjustments. After tasting the dish, they edit their entry to record the result while leaving the original recipe unchanged. A friend registers their own account, and the cook shared the colleciton with them by username. That friend opens the recipe and its cooking entry to understand how the recommended version was made, then adds their own entry after trying it. Both can return to the saved recipe and entries later without searching through screenshots or trying to remember which adjustments worked.
