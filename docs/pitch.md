# Batch

Batch is a recipe tracker for people to store recipes in a consistent format, track changes as they make them, and share recipes with others. Cooks often save the original recipe but lose track of the adjustments that made it turn out well. They also often save recipes in different formats, making them harder to organize and follow. Batch organizes those details and keeps them together so people can recreate dishes they enjoyed and share how they actually made them.

**Recipe Collections**: Firstly, users can save recipes in collections with a consistent format for ingredients and instructions. This makes recipes easier to find and follow without searching through bookmarks, screenshots, and messages. Users can also keep the original source attached so they can return to it and see where the recipe came from.

**Cooking Entries**: Second, users can add cooking entries to a recipe with substitutions, adjustments, and the results. For example, someone making cookies could record that they used less sugar and a longer bake time, along with how they liked the result of that adjustment. Each entry keeps track of who made it and when. The original recipe and previous entries stay available, so trying something new does not erase what worked before.

**Shared Collections**: Lastly, users can share recipe collections with friends and family. Say a user has a collection of recipes they really like, but the versions they enjoyed included adjustments. They can share the collection with those cooking entries attached, so others can see both the original instructions and the changes. Friends and family can then try either version, contribute recipes, and add their own entries about what they changed and how it turned out.
